## What Identipix does

It lets an event organizer share a public Google Drive folder of photos, and lets guests find the photos they appear in using a selfie.

## Using it

- Use it only for lawful purposes and only with photos you have the right to share.
- Organizers must tell guests that face matching is used and must remove photos on request.
- Do not upload or share anyone's selfie or photo without their permission.
- Do not try to break, overload or scrape the service, or to get around its limits.

## Matches are not perfect

Face matching can miss a photo or show one that is not yours. Results are a convenience, not proof of identity. Do not rely on them for anything important.

## Photos stay in Google Drive

Identipix does not host photos. Access to a photo is controlled by the Drive folder's sharing settings. Anyone with the event link can use the "View all photos" link, so only share event links with people who are allowed to see the folder.

## Events end

Events are deleted automatically after 30 days (or sooner by the organizer). We may also remove an event that breaks these terms.
