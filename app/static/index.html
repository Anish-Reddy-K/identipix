Create an event

Paste a public Google Drive folder of event photos. Guests find their own photos with a selfie.

Share the folder as "Anyone with the link".

Photos stay in your Drive. Selfies are never stored.

How to set up your folder
  1. Put your event photos in one Google Drive folder. Subfolders are fine.
  2. Right-click the folder and choose Share.
  3. Under General access, choose Anyone with the link. Leave it on Viewer.
  4. Click Copy link, paste it above, and click Create.

Good to know: up to 3,000 photos per event. Only photos are used (videos are skipped). Photos you add later show up within about 10 minutes. The event is deleted after 30 days.
